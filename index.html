I am a Ph.D. materials engineer with 11 years of experience developing polymer materials and bringing them into mass production. My work connects material formulation, product design, process development, and manufacturing.

I began with bio-based polymers and sustainable materials for automotive applications. At Samsung Electronics, I now lead the adoption of high-performance polymer composites, super engineering plastics, and thermally conductive materials for next-generation hardware.

My experience spans thermal management, lightweight structures, waterproof coatings, and recycling processes, with global manufacturing deployments across Brazil, Vietnam, and India.
