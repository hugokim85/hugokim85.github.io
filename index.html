Yongho (Hugo) Kim

Principal Engineer, Samsung Electronics

Global Technology Research Center · Suwon, South Korea

I am a Ph.D. materials engineer with 11+ years of experience turning materials research into manufacturable products. At Samsung Electronics’ Global Technology Research Center, I work across material formulation, component design, process development, and production validation. My focus is connecting what a material can do in the laboratory with what a product needs in everyday use and high-volume manufacturing.

My current work addresses two closely connected challenges in foldable devices: reducing weight and managing heat within limited space. I lead CFRP-based lightweighting programs through the Galaxy Z Fold8 and am developing a thermal-management component intended for the Galaxy Z Fold9. I have also led FBR-ALD-based quantum-dot durability development for new QLED TVs, alongside material, process, and structural development for new QLED form factors.

Earlier in my career, I developed sustainable polymers for automotive interiors and helped bring waterproof coatings and material-recovery processes into electronics production. Those experiences shaped how I approach new technology: define the product requirement, understand material and process behavior, and work with design teams, suppliers, and manufacturing sites to make the solution practical. My production deployments span Brazil, Vietnam, and India.

Work Experience

Mar 2023 – Present

Principal Engineer

Samsung Electronics · Global Technology Research Center

Suwon, South Korea

  • Foldable-device lightweighting. Lead CFRP material and component development for foldable smartphones, with programs extending through the Galaxy Z Fold8. My work includes display backplates and hinge components, linking composite selection, Siemens NX component and laminate layup design, and molding-process development. Previous developments achieved a 10 g weight reduction and reduced component thickness.
  • Thermal management for foldable devices. Earlier development of thermal-management polymer parts and heat-dissipation resin achieved a 0.7°C reduction in application processor temperature. I am currently developing a separate heat-dissipation component intended for the Galaxy Z Fold9, focusing on thermally conductive materials that spread heat from the application processor. This ongoing development connects material performance with component geometry and the space constraints of a foldable device.
  • Quantum-dot durability and new QLED form factors. Led development using fluidized-bed reactor atomic layer deposition (FBR-ALD) to improve quantum-dot durability for new QLED TVs. Also led materials and structural-design selection, process development, and Product Verification (PV) for new QLED form factors, connecting materials research with product feasibility and manufacturing readiness.
  • From concept to production readiness. Lead new product introduction (NPI) through material validation, process trials, supplier qualification, and EVT/DVT/PVT. Work with product design and manufacturing teams to translate structural, functional, and appearance requirements into practical material and process choices.
  • Experiment-led development. Apply Design of Experiments (DOE), including response surface methodology (RSM) and central composite designs (CCD), to investigate material and process variables. Use Minitab, Python, and AI-assisted material-property simulation and experiment planning to guide trials and reduce unnecessary experimental runs.
  • Supplier collaboration. Co-develop and qualify materials and processes with global suppliers, working through technical evaluation, process capability, and production-readiness requirements so that promising concepts can progress toward high-volume manufacturing.
